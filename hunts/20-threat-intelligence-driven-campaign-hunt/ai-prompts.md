# AI-Assisted Hunt Prompts

> Treat model output as a hypothesis or accelerator. Validate generated fields, queries, assumptions, joins, time ranges, and conclusions against authoritative telemetry.

## Prompt 1

```text
Extract the following from this threat report:
1. Atomic indicators.
2. Behaviors and ATT&CK/ATLAS candidates.
3. Preconditions and target technologies.
4. For each behavior, the local telemetry needed to test it.
5. Claims that are uncertain or vendor-specific.
Do not invent techniques or telemetry. Produce hypotheses, not conclusions.
```
