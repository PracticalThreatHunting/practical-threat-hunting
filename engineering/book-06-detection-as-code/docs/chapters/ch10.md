# Chapter 10: Build Validation Data You Can Trust

This is a cumulative study checkpoint in the edition, not a separate historical release.

## Artifact and proof scope

Case: pristine fixtures exaggerate quality while copied production logs expose private data and have unreliable labels.

Build: small labeled synthetic corpora for DET-001, DET-002, and DET-003; dirty variants; benign lookalikes; manifest; data dictionary; generation recipe; deterministic seed; hashes; provenance; label and label-confidence fields; sanitization policy; licensing/redistribution record; procedure for sampling sanitized production-like data without calling it ground truth. Include enough event-time and ingestion-time variation to exercise DET-002 windows and DET-003 ordering without making fixtures nondeterministic.

Prove: reproducible generation and hashes; schema validation; leak/secret/PII checks; label consistency; deliberately contaminated-data test. Preserve the distinction between synthetic correctness tests and real-world representativeness.

## Exercises and answer criteria

1. Make a controlled one-second timestamp change in a copy of a boundary fixture. Confirm that the corpus hash changes and identify the requirement affected by the edit.
2. Specify a deterministic generator for high-cardinality benign principals while preserving a small known-positive subset. Separate correctness labels from performance distribution assumptions.
3. Design a review process for a sanitized operational sample that cannot be publicly redistributed. Identify its access controls, label limitations, retention, and relationship to the public synthetic corpus. Explain why it should not silently replace the edition fixtures.

The answer records synthetic provenance, seed or explicit construction, immutable hashes, labels, and the limits of production representativeness.
