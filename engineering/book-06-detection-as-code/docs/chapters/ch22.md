# Chapter 22: Coverage with Evidence, Not a Green Heat Map

This is a cumulative study checkpoint in the edition, not a separate historical release.

## Artifact and proof scope

Case: raw ATT&CK tag counts imply protection that has never been validated.

Build: version-pinned coverage ledger with the states applicable, observable, implemented, validated, and operational. Every claim links behavior/ATT&CK strategy or analytic, platform/environment, required telemetry, analytic ID/version, last passing evidence, test type, data-health dependency, owner, confidence, blind spots, and expiry/review date. Produce Navigator as one view only, with an explicit score/color legend.

Prove: identify unsupported claims, stale evidence, single points of failure, redundant independent analytics, and prioritized gaps. Technique-tag counts are inventory, not effectiveness, and one tested procedure does not establish full technique coverage.

## Exercises and answer criteria

1. Downgrade a DET-004 operational claim that has only local fixture evidence. Write the missing evidence needed for each later state.
2. Compare two detections sharing one parser and one enrichment snapshot. Identify common failure points before describing their redundancy.
3. Specify a Navigator legend that represents state without implying measured effectiveness. Explain how stale evidence appears.

The answer pins ATT&CK, names scope and test layer, and narrows unsupported operational claims without deleting legitimate local evidence.
