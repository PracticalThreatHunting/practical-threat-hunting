## Scope

Book 2 treats endpoint and identity as one attack surface. Its 24 hunts cover Windows and PowerShell execution, malware follow-on behavior, process injection, credential theft, persistence, ransomware precursors, remote administration, lateral movement, token theft, Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Kerberos, account abuse, application identities, and directory-control activity.

AI is used as an accelerator for normalization, clustering, timeline construction, query drafting, and investigation notes while preserving evidence, explicit uncertainty, verification gates, and human responsibility for conclusions and action.
