# Framework Reference

Technical snapshot for this edition (verified August 2026).

| Framework | Use it for | Current-edition note |
|---|---|---|
| MITRE ATT&CK | Enterprise and identity adversary behavior | ATT&CK v19 is used in the book. The former Defense Evasion tactic was split into Stealth and Defense Impairment in v19. |
| MITRE ATLAS | Adversary behavior against AI-enabled systems | Used for AI-specific behaviors where traditional ATT&CK is not the best description. |
| OWASP GenAI / Agentic | Application, RAG, model, tool, and agent risk patterns | The book references the 2025 GenAI/LLM Top 10 and 2026 Top 10 for Agentic Applications. |
| NIST AI RMF | Governance and organizational AI-risk context | NIST AI 600-1 remains the GenAI Profile used by this edition; AI RMF 1.0 is being revised. |

Mappings are analytical aids, not proof that a hunt or detection covers an entire technique.
